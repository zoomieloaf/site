Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

The short version: we don't collect your personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no ads, and nothing to sell. Our products work on your device, and your files stay there.

Who we are

ZoomieLoaf is the trading name of Individual Entrepreneur Valerii Pozdniakov, Georgia. In this policy, "ZoomieLoaf", "we" and "us" mean that business. For any privacy question, write to hello@zoomieloaf.com.

This policy covers the website zoomieloaf.com and the products we publish, including the Margin extension for VS Code and compatible editors and our browser extensions.

The website

  • The site is a set of static pages. It uses no analytics, no tracking cookies and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • It is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and may keep it in short-lived logs. We don't use this data to identify you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and delete it when it's no longer needed for that conversation, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Margin

Margin is a Markdown editor that runs inside your code editor (VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf, VSCodium and similar).

  • Your files stay on your device. Margin reads and writes the files you open in your editor. It doesn't upload them anywhere.
  • No telemetry. Margin doesn't collect usage statistics, crash reports or any other data, and doesn't contact our servers.
  • Settings stay in your editor. Margin saves a few preferences in your editor's local storage: the page width you chose for a file, whether it has already asked about opening Markdown files in Margin, and which AI destinations you've agreed to. They never leave your device.
  • AI actions are opt-in, and only run when you start one. The first time you use an AI action, Margin tells you where the text will go and asks for your permission. If you agree:
    • When your editor provides a language model (for example GitHub Copilot in VS Code), Margin sends the selected text and your instruction to that model through your editor. The model provider's terms and privacy policy apply.
    • When it doesn't, Margin opens ChatGPT or Claude in your browser with your prompt in the page address, or copies the prompt for you to paste. That service's terms and privacy policy apply.
    Margin sends nothing to us, and nothing to an AI provider unless you run an AI action and agree.
  • Export works offline. PDF export uses a browser that's already installed on your computer, locally. HTML and copy exports stay on your device.
  • Links you click open in your editor or your browser, like any other link.

Browser extensions

Our browser extensions follow the same rules: they don't collect, sell or share your personal data, use no analytics or tracking, and keep their settings in your browser's storage. If an extension ever needs to handle data in any other way, its Chrome Web Store listing and this policy will say exactly what, why and where it goes before that version is published.

Stores and editors

You get our products from third-party stores such as the Visual Studio Marketplace, Open VSX and the Chrome Web Store, and run them inside third-party software. Those companies may collect data under their own policies, for example install counts or your editor's own telemetry. We don't control that data and don't receive personal data from it.

Donations

If you choose to support us through a service such as Ko-fi, that service processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We may see your name, email address and any message you leave, and use them only to thank you and keep records the law requires.

Children

Our products aren't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data, children's included.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Since we hold almost nothing, usually the answer is "nothing", but write to hello@zoomieloaf.com and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Georgia, that's the Personal Data Protection Service.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how a product handles your data, we'll say so in that product's release notes.
